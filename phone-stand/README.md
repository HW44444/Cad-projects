# Phone Stand with Charging Port Cutout

A desk stand that holds my phone at a viewing angle while it charges.

## Why I built it
I wanted a stand that props my phone up for watching and video calls, but most simple stands block the charging port so you can't use the phone while it's plugged in. I designed mine with a cutout at the base so the cable passes straight through.

## Design details
- Angled back support so the phone leans at a comfortable viewing angle
- Front lip deep enough to hold the phone with its case on
- Cutout at the base for a charging cable to pass through while docked
- Filleted edges for a finished feel and added strength
- Modeled in Autodesk Fusion

## Files
- `phone-stand.step` — universal CAD format, editable in any CAD program
- `phone-stand.stl` — mesh file, ready to slice and 3D print

## What I learned
- **Design around the whole use case, not just the object.** A stand that holds a phone is easy; a stand that holds a phone *while it charges* required thinking about the cable as part of the design.
- **Print orientation affects strength.** Printed standing upright, the shelf would be a weak overhang needing support material. Laid flat on its back, it prints stronger with no supports.
- **Real-world clearances.** The lip has to fit the phone plus its case, not just the phone.
- **Fillets aren't only cosmetic** — rounding internal corners removes the stress concentrations where parts tend to crack.

## Print notes
- Lay the stand flat on its back on the build plate
- No supports needed in that orientation
- PLA is fine for this
